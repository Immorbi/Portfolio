Case 04

Editorial Design / The Gallery

A recurring publication system for The Gallery's social presence — two editorial formats, each with its own visual logic, built inside one coherent identity.

Client The Gallery
Tbilisi, Georgia
Role Art direction
Graphic design
Scope Social media
Editorial system
Year 2025 — 2026
Goal

The Gallery needed a social media presence that felt as considered as the space itself. Not a generic art account. The content had to carry two very different formats — in-depth artist interviews and personal artist spotlights — without looking like it was assembled from a template.

Task

Design two recurring editorial formats for social media: a structured interview series and an artist spotlight format. Each needed its own visual logic while staying recognisably part of the same identity.

Solution

Each series gets its own typographic treatment and colour register. The interview series leans on bold display type and a dark palette. The artist features use a lighter, more graphic approach with a per-subject colour accent. Together they read as one system.

01 — Interview series

5 Questions for the Artist.

A recurring interview format built around five questions per artist. Each series opens with a cover card — the artist's portrait with their name as the dominant element — and continues with question/answer cards interspersed with artwork photography.

Two colour registers: charcoal for Polina Orlova, navy for Tasia K.

Polina Orlova

02 — Artist spotlight

What's Your Inspiration.

A lighter series focused on individual artists connected to the gallery. Each post gets a distinct colour accent applied to the artist's name set in a custom display treatment.

The palette shifts per subject — pink for Nino Gogolidze, purple for Mako Lomadze — while the layout logic stays constant across the series.
