The idea

DIRTALK is a podcast and media platform built for conversations that matter. It's a space where founders, leaders, and makers speak candidly about the real costs of success.

The brand was built on a simple premise: authentic conversations happen when people feel free to speak openly. Away from scripts, performance, and polished narratives, real stories emerge. DIRTALK exists at the intersection of business, culture, and personal truth, designed to move beyond headlines and uncover deeper realities.

The identity reflects this directness. A tight, confident wordmark paired with a signature four-point star. Monospace captions. Stark photography. Electric blue holds the system, bold enough to command attention across feeds and formats, disciplined enough to stay editorial. Every element reinforces the brand's commitment to honesty and clarity.

More than a podcast, DIRTALK is a media platform designed to spark curiosity, encourage meaningful dialogue, and amplify voices that deserve to be heard. It's where stories are told honestly, perspectives are shared openly, and conversations uncover what matters most.
