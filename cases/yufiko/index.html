01 — Brief

Posters for Yufiko Market events.

Yufiko is a street food bar and market space in Tbilisi that hosts local makers, artists, and vintage curators. What started as a poster for their open vendor call grew into an ongoing series of event posters — DJ sets, prepartys, and market announcements.

Each event gets its own visual voice while staying recognizably Yufiko. The latest — Sundown Session, a live DJ set planned for summer 2026.
