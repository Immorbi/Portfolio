01 — Brief

Vendor call for Yufiko Market.

Yufiko is a street food bar and market space in Tbilisi that hosts local makers, artists, and vintage curators. They needed a poster for their open vendor call — something that felt warm, inviting, and true to their community-driven character.

Five visual directions were explored before landing on the one that felt most like Yufiko.
